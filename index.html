Overdue Street Light Finder

For the NYC DOT Manhattan street lighting manager: see which "Street Light Out" requests are past the 10-day target, in priority order, for the weekly review.

1 Load requests

Upload a 311 export as a CSV file. It needs these columns: Unique Key, Created Date, Closed Date and Status. Other columns are ignored.

Your file stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Sample data: NYC Open Data 311, DOT, Street Light Out, Manhattan, created July 1 to August 30, 2026 (every 3rd request).

2 Check the rule

10 days is the DOT response target. "Due soon" means an open request that will pass the target within 3 days.

3 Review the priority list

Requests are sorted by how long they have been open, oldest first. Pick your note (next action) for each one. Your note does not change the official 311 status. Notes are saved in this browser only.

Know the limits

  • "Closed" in 311 means DOT closed the case on paper. It does not prove the light was fixed.
  • The tool only sorts and flags. A person decides what to do with each request.
  • The 311 data does not say why a request is stuck. Use the review note (for example "Waiting on Con Edison") to record that.
  • Do not upload files that contain names, phone numbers or other personal details.
